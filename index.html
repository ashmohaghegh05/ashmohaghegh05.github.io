About Me

I am currently in the process of applying to graduate programs in urban and city planning and design.

My interests sit at the intersection of technology, entrepreneurship, design, and the built environment. I am particularly interested in how thoughtful planning, innovation, and emerging technologies can contribute to more functional, sustainable, and engaging cities.
